We're working to submit a paper on this topic right now, and I'll be updating this page as soon as that's done. Here's the punchline: we WAY overstudy birds and mammals, primarily at a cost to insects and arthropods in general. This is probably because people like furry cute things better than they like chitinous 6+ legged things.
